About Energle, the daily energy game

Energle is a free daily geography puzzle about electricity. Each day it shows one country’s energy profile: how much electricity it generates and uses per person, what it imports and exports, and how its power mix of coal, gas, nuclear, hydro, wind and solar has changed since 2000. You have six guesses to name the country. After each miss, an arrow and a distance point you closer, in the spirit of Wordle and Worldle.

Stuck? The Clue Shop trades a guess for a clue card: renewable potential, oil, gas and mineral reserves, fuel and electricity trade partners, and how people live. Once you have played, Explore puts every country on an interactive globe: add up a continent, the EU or OPEC as if it were one country, or colour the world by nuclear, coal or solar power.

Energle runs on open data: Ember’s yearly electricity data, with the World Bank, EIA, OPEC, USGS, NASA, NOAA, ENTSO-E, CEPII and the CIA World Factbook, refreshed automatically every month. It is made by Oscar Viguié as a fun way to learn about the world’s energy and the energy transition.

Is Energle free?
Yes. No sign-up and no download: it works in any browser, on a phone or a computer.
When is there a new puzzle?
Every day at midnight UTC. Every past puzzle stays playable in the Archive.
Who is it for?
Anyone curious about energy, geography or climate: students, teachers, energy professionals and puzzle fans.
