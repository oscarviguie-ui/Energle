How Guyana makes its electricity

Guyana generated about 1.4 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 95% came from other fossil fuels. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy
  • Gas

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 640 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Guyana makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
