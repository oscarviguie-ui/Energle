How Portugal makes its electricity

Portugal generated about 51 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 30% came from hydro, and about a quarter from wind. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 80% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 130 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about a third to almost nothing, wind rose from almost nothing to about a quarter and solar rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Portugal is a net importer: about 15% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Spain.
  • Main fuel supplier: Brazil.
