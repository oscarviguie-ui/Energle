How Paraguay makes its electricity

Paraguay generated about 45 TWh of electricity in 2025. Almost all of it came from hydro.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 20 grams of CO₂, less than a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Paraguay is a net exporter: it sells about 35% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Brazil, Argentina.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
