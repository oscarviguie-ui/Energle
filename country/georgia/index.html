How Georgia makes its electricity

Georgia generated about 14 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 80% came from hydro, and about 20% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 80% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Wind

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 150 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Georgia is a net importer: about 8% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Azerbaijan, Türkiye, Armenia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Azerbaijan.
