How Zimbabwe makes its electricity

Zimbabwe generated about 10 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 55% came from hydro, and about 45% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 55% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a fifth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 380 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 55% to about 45% and hydro rose from about 45% to about 55% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Zimbabwe is a net importer: about 15% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 85% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: South Africa, Mozambique, Zambia.
  • Main fuel supplier: the Bahamas.
