How Uzbekistan makes its electricity

Uzbekistan generated about 87 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 70% came from gas, and about 7% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Wind
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about two-thirds of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 1000 grams of CO₂, about twice the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 10% to about 2%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 7% and gas fell from about 80% to about 70% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Uzbekistan is a net importer: about 3% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a fifth of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Turkmenistan, Afghanistan, Tajikistan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
