How Kazakhstan makes its electricity

Kazakhstan generated about 120 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 55% came from coal, and about 30% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 810 grams of CO₂, about twice the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from about 10% to about 30%, coal fell from about 70% to about 55% and hydro fell from about 15% to about 8% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Kazakhstan is a net importer: about 2% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Russia, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
