How Zambia makes its electricity

Zambia generated about 20 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 85% came from hydro, and about 10% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses about a fifth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 130 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about 85% and coal rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Zambia is a net exporter: it sells about 15% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 80% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Namibia, Botswana, DR Congo.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
