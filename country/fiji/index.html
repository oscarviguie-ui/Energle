How Fiji makes its electricity

Fiji generated about 1.1 TWh of electricity in 2024. About half came from hydro, and about 35% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 65% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 280 grams of CO₂, about two-thirds of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels rose from about 30% to about 35% and bioenergy fell from about 15% to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Fiji makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
