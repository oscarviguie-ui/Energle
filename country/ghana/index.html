How Ghana makes its electricity

Ghana generated about 24 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 60% came from gas, and about a third from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 35% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 470 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from almost nothing to about 60%, hydro fell from about 90% to about a third and other fossil fuels fell from about 9% to about 2% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Ghana is a net exporter: it sells about 10% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Burkina Faso.
  • Main fuel supplier: the Netherlands.
