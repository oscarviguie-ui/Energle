How China makes its electricity

China generated about 11,000 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 55% came from coal, and about 15% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 40% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Nuclear
  • Gas
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about twice the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 530 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 80% to about 55%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 10% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

China makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Hong Kong, Macao, Myanmar.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
