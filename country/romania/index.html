How Romania makes its electricity

Romania generated about 50 TWh of electricity in 2025. About a quarter came from hydro, and about 20% from nuclear. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about two-thirds of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Nuclear
  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 260 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 35% to about 15%, wind rose from almost nothing to about 10% and nuclear rose from about 10% to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Romania is a net importer: about 7% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Bulgaria, Hungary, Ukraine.
  • Main fuel supplier: Kazakhstan.
