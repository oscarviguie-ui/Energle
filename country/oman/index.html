How Oman makes its electricity

Oman generated about 53 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 90% came from gas. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about 2.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 540 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 15% to about 4% and gas rose from about 85% to about 90% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Oman makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a tenth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: India.
