How India makes its electricity

India generated about 2,100 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 70% came from coal, and about 9% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a quarter of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Nuclear
  • Gas
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 670 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, solar rose from almost nothing to about 9% and gas fell from about 10% to about 2% of the mix.

Trade and resources

India makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
