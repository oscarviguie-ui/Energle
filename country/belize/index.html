How Belize makes its electricity

Belize generated about 0.5 TWh of electricity in 2024. About half came from hydro, and about a third from bioenergy. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 170 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, bioenergy rose from almost nothing to about a third, hydro fell from about 70% to about half and other fossil fuels fell from about 30% to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Belize is a net importer: about 45% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
