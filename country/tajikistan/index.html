How Tajikistan makes its electricity

Tajikistan generated about 24 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 95% came from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 95% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Gas

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 70 grams of CO₂, about a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Tajikistan is a net exporter: it sells about 20% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Uzbekistan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Uzbekistan.
