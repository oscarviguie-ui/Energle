How Mauritius makes its electricity

Mauritius generated about 3.4 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 45% came from other fossil fuels, and about 40% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Coal
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Hydro

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 640 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal rose from about 20% to about 40%, bioenergy fell from about a quarter to about 9% and other fossil fuels fell from about half to about 45% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Mauritius makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United Arab Emirates.
