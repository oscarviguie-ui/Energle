How Lebanon makes its electricity

Lebanon generated about 5.3 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 55% came from other fossil fuels, and about 30% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 45% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a quarter of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 390 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 95% to about 55%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 30% and hydro rose from about 4% to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Lebanon makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Greece.
