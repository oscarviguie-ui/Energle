How Sri Lanka makes its electricity

Sri Lanka generated about 17 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from hydro, and about a quarter from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 65% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a fifth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 320 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 55% to about 10%, coal rose from almost nothing to about a quarter and solar rose from almost nothing to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Sri Lanka makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
