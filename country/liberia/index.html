How Liberia makes its electricity

Liberia generated about 0.6 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 55% came from hydro, and about 45% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 55% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 320 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about 45% and hydro rose from almost nothing to about 55% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Liberia makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 55% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: China.
