How Bhutan makes its electricity

Bhutan generated about 11 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from hydro.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro

Each person uses about 4 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 20 grams of CO₂, less than a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Bhutan is a net importer: about 10% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: India.
  • Main fuel supplier: India.
