How Honduras makes its electricity

Honduras generated about 12 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 45% came from other fossil fuels, and about 30% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 55% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy
  • Wind
  • Other Renewables

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 320 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about 60% to about 30%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 9% and bioenergy rose from almost nothing to about 7% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Honduras is a net importer: about 1% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Guatemala.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
