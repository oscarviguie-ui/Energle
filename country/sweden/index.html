How Sweden makes its electricity

Sweden generated about 170 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from hydro, and about a quarter from nuclear. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied almost all of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Nuclear
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about 3.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 40 grams of CO₂, less than a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, wind rose from almost nothing to about a quarter, hydro fell from about 55% to about 40% and nuclear fell from about 40% to about a quarter of the mix.

Trade and resources

Sweden is a net exporter: it sells about 20% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about half the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Finland, Norway, Denmark.
  • Main fuel supplier: Norway.
