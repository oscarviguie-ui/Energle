How Rwanda makes its electricity

Rwanda generated about 1.1 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 45% came from hydro, and about a quarter from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about half of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 350 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 40% to about 9%, gas rose from almost nothing to about a quarter and hydro fell from about 60% to about 45% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Rwanda is a net importer: about 7% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 90% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Uganda.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Korea.
