How Serbia makes its electricity

Serbia generated about 37 TWh of electricity in 2025. About two-thirds came from coal, and about 20% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 30% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 700 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about 30% to about 20% and gas rose from about 1% to about 7% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Serbia is a net importer: about 3% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Romania, Bulgaria, Hungary.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
