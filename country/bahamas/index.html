How the Bahamas makes its electricity

The Bahamas generated about 2.2 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from other fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 650 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

The Bahamas makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: a little more than the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
