How Russia makes its electricity

Russia generated about 1,200 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 45% came from gas, and about 20% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a third of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Nuclear
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about twice the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 450 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Russia is a net exporter: it sells about 1% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about half the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a third of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Estonia, Latvia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Kazakhstan.
