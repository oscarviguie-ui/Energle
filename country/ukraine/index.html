How Ukraine makes its electricity

Ukraine generated about 110 TWh of electricity in 2022. About 55% came from nuclear, and about 20% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 70% of the total.

The mix in 2022

  • Nuclear
  • Coal
  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 250 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 15% to about 6%, nuclear rose from about 45% to about 55% and coal fell from about 30% to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Ukraine makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Slovakia, Romania, Hungary.
  • Main fuel supplier: Poland.
