How Bulgaria makes its electricity

Bulgaria generated about 38 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from nuclear, and about a quarter from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 70% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Nuclear
  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Bioenergy
  • Wind
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 280 grams of CO₂, about two-thirds of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 40% to about a quarter, solar rose from almost nothing to about 20% and nuclear fell from about 45% to about 40% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Bulgaria is a net exporter: it sells about 3% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Romania, Greece, Serbia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Kazakhstan.
