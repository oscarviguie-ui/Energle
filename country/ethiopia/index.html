How Ethiopia makes its electricity

Ethiopia generated about 33 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 95% came from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied almost all of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Wind

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 20 grams of CO₂, less than a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Ethiopia is a net exporter: it sells about 7% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Kenya.
  • Main fuel supplier: China.
