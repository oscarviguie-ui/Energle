How Austria makes its electricity

Austria generated about 73 TWh of electricity in 2025. About half came from hydro, and about 15% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about twice the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 120 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about 70% to about half, solar rose from almost nothing to about 15% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Austria is a net importer: about 5% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Germany, Czechia, Slovenia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Germany.
