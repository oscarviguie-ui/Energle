How Italy makes its electricity

Italy generated about 260 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 45% came from gas, and about 15% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about half of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas

  • Wind
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil
  • Other Renewables
  • Coal

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 280 grams of CO₂, about two-thirds of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about a third to about 3%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 15% and gas rose from about 40% to about 45% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Italy is a net importer: about 15% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: France, Switzerland, Slovenia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Algeria.
