How Armenia makes its electricity

Armenia generated about 8.6 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from gas, and about a third from nuclear. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 60% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Nuclear
  • Hydro
  • Solar

Each person uses about two-thirds of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 240 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, solar rose from almost nothing to about 8% and gas fell from about 45% to about 40% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Armenia is a net exporter: it sells about 20% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Iran, Georgia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
