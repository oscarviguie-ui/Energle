How Brazil makes its electricity

Brazil generated about 750 TWh of electricity in 2025. About half came from hydro, and about 15% from wind. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 90% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Wind

  • Bioenergy
  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Nuclear
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 110 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about 85% to about half, wind rose from almost nothing to about 15% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Brazil is a net importer: about 2% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest power links: Paraguay, Argentina, Uruguay.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
