How Benin makes its electricity

Benin generated about 1 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 70% came from gas, and about a quarter from other fossil fuels. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 580 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about a quarter and gas rose from almost nothing to about 70% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Benin is a net importer: about 55% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 70% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Nigeria.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
