How Syria makes its electricity

Syria generated about 25 TWh of electricity in 2024. About half came from gas, and about 45% from other fossil fuels. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro

Each person uses about a quarter of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 710 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from about 35% to about half, hydro fell from about 15% to about 3% and other fossil fuels fell from about half to about 45% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Syria is a net exporter: it sells about 2% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Türkiye.
