How Gabon makes its electricity

Gabon generated about 2.6 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 45% came from gas, and about a third from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a third of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 520 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from about 20% to about 45% and hydro fell from about 60% to about a third of the mix.

Trade and resources

Gabon is a net importer: about 20% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Belgium.
