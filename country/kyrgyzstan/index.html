How Kyrgyzstan makes its electricity

Kyrgyzstan generated about 16 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 85% came from hydro, and about 10% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 150 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 9% to about 2% and coal rose from about 5% to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Kyrgyzstan is a net importer: about 20% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
