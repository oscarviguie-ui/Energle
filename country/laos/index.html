How Laos makes its electricity

Laos generated about 52 TWh of electricity in 2024. About three-quarters came from hydro, and about a quarter from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Coal

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 230 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal rose from almost nothing to about a quarter, hydro fell from about 90% to about three-quarters and other fossil fuels fell from about 10% to almost nothing of the mix.

Trade and resources

Laos is a net exporter: it sells about 70% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Thailand, Cambodia, China.
  • Main fuel supplier: Thailand.
