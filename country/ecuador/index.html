How Ecuador makes its electricity

Ecuador generated about 37 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 80% came from hydro, and about 20% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 80% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Gas
  • Bioenergy
  • Wind

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 160 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 30% to about 20% and hydro rose from about 70% to about 80% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Ecuador makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Colombia.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
