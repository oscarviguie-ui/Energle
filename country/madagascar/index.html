How Madagascar makes its electricity

Madagascar generated about 2.4 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 35% came from other fossil fuels, and about 35% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 40% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 430 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about 65% to about 35% and coal rose from almost nothing to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Madagascar makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 75% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Oman.
