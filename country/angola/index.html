How Angola makes its electricity

Angola generated about 16 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 70% came from hydro, and about 15% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Gas
  • Solar

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 190 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 35% to about 15%, gas rose from almost nothing to about 9% and hydro rose from about 65% to about 70% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Angola makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 80% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Korea.
