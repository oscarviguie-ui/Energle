How Peru makes its electricity

Peru generated about 60 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 55% came from hydro, and about 35% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 65% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 240 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from about 4% to about 35%, hydro fell from about 80% to about 55% and other fossil fuels fell from about 10% to almost nothing of the mix.

Trade and resources

Peru makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
