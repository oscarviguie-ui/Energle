How Kosovo makes its electricity

Kosovo generated about 5.9 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 90% came from coal, and about 7% from wind. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 10% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Wind
  • Hydro

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 900 grams of CO₂, about twice the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from almost all to about 90% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 7% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Kosovo is a net importer: about 20% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.
