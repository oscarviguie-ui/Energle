How Switzerland makes its electricity

Switzerland generated about 65 TWh of electricity in 2025. About half came from hydro, and about 30% from nuclear. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied almost all of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Renewables

Each person uses about twice the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 40 grams of CO₂, less than a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, solar rose from almost nothing to about 10% and nuclear fell from about 40% to about 30% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Switzerland makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Italy, France, Germany.
  • Main fuel supplier: Germany.
