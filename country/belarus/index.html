How Belarus makes its electricity

Belarus generated about 46 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 55% came from gas, and about 35% from nuclear. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 40% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Nuclear
  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Wind

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 310 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 95% to about 55% and nuclear rose from almost nothing to about 35% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Belarus is a net exporter: it sells about 2% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Lithuania.
  • Main fuel supplier: Germany.
