How Cyprus makes its electricity

Cyprus generated about 5.9 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 70% came from other fossil fuels, and about a quarter from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 30% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 490 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about 70% and solar rose from almost nothing to about a quarter of the mix.

Trade and resources

Cyprus makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Türkiye.
