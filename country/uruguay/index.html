How Uruguay makes its electricity

Uruguay generated about 12 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from wind, and about a quarter from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied almost all of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Wind
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 80 grams of CO₂, about a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about 95% to about a quarter, wind rose from almost nothing to about 40% and bioenergy rose from almost nothing to about a quarter of the mix.

Trade and resources

Uruguay is a net importer: about 20% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: a little more than the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Argentina, Brazil.
  • Main fuel supplier: Nigeria.
