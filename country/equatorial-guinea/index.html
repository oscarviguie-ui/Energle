How Equatorial Guinea makes its electricity

Equatorial Guinea generated about 1.5 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 70% came from gas, and about a quarter from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a quarter of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about a fifth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 640 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 20% to almost nothing, gas rose from about 60% to about 70% and hydro rose from about 20% to about a quarter of the mix.

Trade and resources

Equatorial Guinea makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 90% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Côte d'Ivoire.
