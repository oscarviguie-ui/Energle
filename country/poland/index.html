How Poland makes its electricity

Poland generated about 170 TWh of electricity in 2025. About half came from coal, and about 15% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a third of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro

Each person uses a little more than the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 590 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 95% to about half, wind rose from almost nothing to about 15% and gas rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Poland makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Germany, Czechia, Slovakia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Saudi Arabia.
