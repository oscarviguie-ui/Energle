How El Salvador makes its electricity

El Salvador generated about 6.5 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 30% came from hydro, and about 20% from other renewables. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Other Renewables
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy
  • Wind

Each person uses about a quarter of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 140 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 45% to about 15%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 20% and bioenergy rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

El Salvador is a net exporter: it sells about 3% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Guatemala.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
