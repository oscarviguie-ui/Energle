How Turkmenistan makes its electricity

Turkmenistan generated about 33 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from gas.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 1310 grams of CO₂, about 3 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Turkmenistan is a net exporter: it sells about 25% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a tenth of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Uzbekistan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Germany.
