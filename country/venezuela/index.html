How Venezuela makes its electricity

Venezuela generated about 80 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 90% came from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 90% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 90 grams of CO₂, about a fifth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro rose from about three-quarters to about 90% and gas fell from about 15% to about 4% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Venezuela makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
