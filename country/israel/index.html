How Israel makes its electricity

Israel generated about 82 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 80% came from gas, and about 15% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Coal
  • Wind

Each person uses about twice the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 490 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from almost nothing to about 80%, coal fell from about two-thirds to about 3% and other fossil fuels fell from about a third to almost nothing of the mix.

Trade and resources

Israel is a net exporter: it sells about 8% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a third of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Azerbaijan.
