How Moldova makes its electricity

Moldova generated about 2.9 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 65% came from gas, and about 10% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 30% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 510 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 90% to about 65%, wind rose from almost nothing to about 8% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 8% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Moldova is a net importer: about 45% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a third of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Romania, Ukraine.
  • Main fuel supplier: Romania.
