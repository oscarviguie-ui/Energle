How Cuba makes its electricity

Cuba generated about 19 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 80% came from other fossil fuels, and about 15% from gas. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Gas
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Hydro

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 640 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Cuba makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: a little more than the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Italy.
