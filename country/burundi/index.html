How Burundi makes its electricity

Burundi generated about 0.5 TWh of electricity in 2024. About three-quarters came from hydro, and about a quarter from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 180 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about three-quarters and other fossil fuels rose from almost nothing to about a quarter of the mix.

Trade and resources

Burundi is a net importer: about 15% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 70% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: a little more than the world average.
