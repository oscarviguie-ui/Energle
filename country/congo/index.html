How Congo makes its electricity

Congo generated about 5.5 TWh of electricity in 2024. About three-quarters came from gas, and about 20% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a fifth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 720 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about 20%, gas rose from almost nothing to about three-quarters and other fossil fuels rose from almost nothing to about 5% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Congo is a net exporter: it sells about 1% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 70% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Africa.
