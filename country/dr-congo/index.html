How DR Congo makes its electricity

DR Congo generated about 16 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 85% came from hydro, and about 15% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied almost all of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 30 grams of CO₂, less than a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about 85% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

DR Congo is a net importer: about 8% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 55% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Zambia, Mozambique, Zimbabwe.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Africa.
