How Malta makes its electricity

Malta generated about 2.2 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 85% came from gas, and about 15% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 480 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from almost all to almost nothing, gas rose from almost nothing to about 85% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Malta is a net importer: about 30% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Italy.
  • Main fuel supplier: Italy.
