How Eswatini makes its electricity

Eswatini generated about 0.6 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 55% came from hydro, and about a third from bioenergy. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 95% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Coal

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 130 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, bioenergy fell from about 70% to about a third and hydro rose from about 30% to about 55% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Eswatini is a net importer: about 65% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 90% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: South Africa, Mozambique.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Africa.
