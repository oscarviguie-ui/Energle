How Pakistan makes its electricity

Pakistan generated about 180 TWh of electricity in 2025. About a quarter came from hydro, and about 20% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 60% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Coal
  • Nuclear
  • Wind
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about a fifth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 310 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 40% to almost nothing, solar rose from almost nothing to about 20% and coal rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Pakistan makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a third of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Iran.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United Arab Emirates.
