How Myanmar makes its electricity

Myanmar generated about 22 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 45% came from gas, and about 45% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about half of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 500 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 15% to almost nothing, hydro rose from about 35% to about 45% and coal rose from almost nothing to about 6% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Myanmar makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: China, Thailand.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
