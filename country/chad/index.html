How Chad makes its electricity

Chad generated about 0.4 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 95% came from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 5% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 620 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about 95% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Chad makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 50% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Niger.
