How Uganda makes its electricity

Uganda generated about 5.8 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 85% came from hydro, and about 8% from bioenergy. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied almost all of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 60 grams of CO₂, about a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about 85% and bioenergy rose from about 2% to about 8% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Uganda is a net exporter: it sells about 8% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 80% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United Arab Emirates.
