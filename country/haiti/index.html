How Haiti makes its electricity

Haiti generated about 0.9 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 80% came from other fossil fuels, and about 20% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 530 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels rose from about half to about 80% and hydro fell from about half to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Haiti makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 85% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
