How Suriname makes its electricity

Suriname generated about 1.7 TWh of electricity in 2024. About half came from hydro, and about 45% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 55% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 320 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about three-quarters to about half and other fossil fuels rose from about a quarter to about 45% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Suriname makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
