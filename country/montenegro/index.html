How Montenegro makes its electricity

Montenegro generated about 2.5 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 60% came from hydro, and about a quarter from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Wind
  • Solar

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 260 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2005

Since 2005, wind rose from almost nothing to about 10%, coal fell from about a third to about a quarter and hydro fell from about two-thirds to about 60% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Montenegro is a net importer: about 30% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Bosnia and Herzegovina, Italy, Albania.
  • Main fuel supplier: Greece.
