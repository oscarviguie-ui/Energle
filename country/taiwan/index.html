How Taiwan makes its electricity

Taiwan generated about 290 TWh of electricity in 2025. About half came from gas, and about 35% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Nuclear
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about 3 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 630 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from about 10% to about half, nuclear fell from about 20% to about 1% and other fossil fuels fell from about 15% to about 2% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Taiwan makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.
