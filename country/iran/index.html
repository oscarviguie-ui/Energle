How Iran makes its electricity

Iran generated about 400 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 90% came from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 6% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Nuclear

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 660 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 30% to about 5% and gas rose from about two-thirds to about 90% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Iran makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a fifth of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Pakistan, Armenia, Azerbaijan.
  • Main fuel supplier: China.
