How the Central African Republic makes its electricity

The Central African Republic generated about 0.1 TWh of electricity in 2023. Almost all of it came from hydro.

The mix in 2023

  • Hydro

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro rose from about 80% to almost all and other fossil fuels fell from about 20% to almost nothing of the mix.

Trade and resources

The Central African Republic makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 40% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: a little more than the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Cameroon.
