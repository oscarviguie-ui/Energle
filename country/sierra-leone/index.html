How Sierra Leone makes its electricity

Sierra Leone generated about 0.2 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 85% came from hydro, and about 10% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 95% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 50 grams of CO₂, about a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 90% to about 5%, hydro rose from about 10% to about 85% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Sierra Leone makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 55% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Spain.
