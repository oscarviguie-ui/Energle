How Botswana makes its electricity

Botswana generated about 3.4 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from coal.

The mix in 2024

  • Coal
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 850 grams of CO₂, about twice the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Botswana is a net importer: about 25% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a third of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: South Africa, Zambia, Mozambique.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Africa.
