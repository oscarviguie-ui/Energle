How Djibouti makes its electricity

Djibouti generated about 0.2 TWh of electricity in 2024. About two-thirds came from other fossil fuels, and about a third from wind. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a third of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Wind

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 450 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, wind rose from almost nothing to about a third and other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about two-thirds of the mix.

Trade and resources

Djibouti is a net importer: about 75% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 75% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a fifth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: India.
