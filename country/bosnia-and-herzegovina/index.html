How Bosnia and Herzegovina makes its electricity

Bosnia and Herzegovina generated about 15 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 55% came from coal, and about 35% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 45% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 570 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about half to about 35% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Bosnia and Herzegovina is a net exporter: it sells about 15% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Croatia, Montenegro, Serbia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Italy.
