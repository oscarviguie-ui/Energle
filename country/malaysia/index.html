How Malaysia makes its electricity

Malaysia generated about 200 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 45% came from coal, and about a third from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 600 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 80% to about a third, coal rose from about 6% to about 45% and hydro rose from about 10% to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Malaysia makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Indonesia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
