How Morocco makes its electricity

Morocco generated about 44 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 60% came from coal, and about 15% from wind. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a quarter of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Wind
  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 600 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about a quarter to about 4%, wind rose from almost nothing to about 15% and gas rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Morocco is a net importer: about 8% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a third of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Spain.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
