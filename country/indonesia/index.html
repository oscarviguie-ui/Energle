How Indonesia makes its electricity

Indonesia generated about 370 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 60% came from coal, and about 20% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Renewables
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 680 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal rose from about a third to about 60%, gas fell from about a third to about 20% and other fossil fuels fell from about 15% to about 2% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Indonesia makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Malaysia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
