How Burkina Faso makes its electricity

Burkina Faso generated about 1.7 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 85% came from other fossil fuels, and about 7% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 560 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about a quarter to about 5%, other fossil fuels rose from about three-quarters to about 85% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 7% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Burkina Faso is a net importer: about 45% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 90% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
