How Slovakia makes its electricity

Slovakia generated about 29 TWh of electricity in 2025. About two-thirds came from nuclear, and about 10% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Nuclear
  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Coal

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 90 grams of CO₂, about a fifth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 20% to about 1%, nuclear rose from about 55% to about two-thirds and bioenergy rose from almost nothing to about 5% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Slovakia is a net exporter: it sells about 8% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Hungary, Czechia, Poland.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
