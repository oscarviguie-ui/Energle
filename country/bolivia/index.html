How Bolivia makes its electricity

Bolivia generated about 14 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 60% came from gas, and about 30% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 35% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about a quarter of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 480 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about half to about 30% and gas rose from about half to about 60% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Bolivia makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Argentina.
