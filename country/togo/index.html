How Togo makes its electricity

Togo generated about 0.7 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 70% came from gas, and about 20% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 30% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Hydro

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 420 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from almost nothing to about 70%, other fossil fuels fell from about 45% to almost nothing and hydro fell from about 55% to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Togo is a net importer: about 70% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Nigeria.
  • Main fuel supplier: India.
