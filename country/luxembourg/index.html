How Luxembourg makes its electricity

Luxembourg generated about 1.5 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 30% came from wind, and about 30% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 90% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil
  • Gas
  • Hydro

Each person uses about 2.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 120 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about half to about 4%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 30% and hydro fell from about 30% to about 3% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Luxembourg is a net importer: about 75% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Germany, Czechia, Belgium.
  • Main fuel supplier: Belgium.
