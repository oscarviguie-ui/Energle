How France makes its electricity

France generated about 570 TWh of electricity in 2025. About two-thirds came from nuclear, and about 10% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 95% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Nuclear
  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about twice the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 40 grams of CO₂, about a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, nuclear fell from about 80% to about two-thirds, wind rose from almost nothing to about 8% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 6% of the mix.

Trade and resources

France is a net exporter: it sells about 15% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Italy, the United Kingdom, Germany.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
