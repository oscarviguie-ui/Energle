How Brunei makes its electricity

Brunei generated about 5.6 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 80% came from gas, and about 20% from coal. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about 3 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 890 grams of CO₂, about twice the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from almost all to about 80% and coal rose from almost nothing to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Brunei makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Malaysia.
