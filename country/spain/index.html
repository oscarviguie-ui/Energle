How Spain makes its electricity

Spain generated about 290 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 20% came from solar, and about 20% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Wind
  • Nuclear
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 150 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about a third to almost nothing, solar rose from almost nothing to about 20% and wind rose from about 2% to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Spain is a net exporter: it sells about 4% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: France, Portugal.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
