How Palestine makes its electricity

Palestine generated about 1 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 60% came from other fossil fuels, and about 40% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 40% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 410 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, solar rose from almost nothing to about 40% and other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about 60% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Palestine is a net importer: about 90% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a third of the world average.
