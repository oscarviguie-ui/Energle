How Singapore makes its electricity

Singapore generated about 60 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 90% came from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 5% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Coal

Each person uses about 2.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 500 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 80% to about 2% and gas rose from about 20% to about 90% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Singapore makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United Arab Emirates.
