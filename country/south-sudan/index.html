How South Sudan makes its electricity

South Sudan generated about 0.6 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from other fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 640 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2012

The mix has changed little since 2012.

Trade and resources

South Sudan makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 25% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Kenya.
