How the Gambia makes its electricity

The Gambia generated about 0.5 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from other fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 670 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

The Gambia makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 85% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
