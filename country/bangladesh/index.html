How Bangladesh makes its electricity

Bangladesh generated about 100 TWh of electricity in 2025. About two-thirds came from gas, and about 20% from coal. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Hydro

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 700 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 90% to about two-thirds, coal rose from almost nothing to about 20% and other fossil fuels rose from about 6% to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Bangladesh is a net importer: about 10% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: India.
  • Main fuel supplier: China.
