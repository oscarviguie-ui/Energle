How Jamaica makes its electricity

Jamaica generated about 4.9 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 55% came from gas, and about 30% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 560 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 95% to about 30%, gas rose from almost nothing to about 55% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 6% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Jamaica makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
