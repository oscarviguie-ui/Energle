How Guinea-Bissau makes its electricity

Guinea-Bissau generated about 0.1 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from other fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 620 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Guinea-Bissau makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 65% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Portugal.
