How North Korea makes its electricity

North Korea generated about 27 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 65% came from hydro, and about a third from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 65% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses about a quarter of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 340 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro rose from about half to about 65% and coal fell from about 45% to about a third of the mix.

Trade and resources

North Korea makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
