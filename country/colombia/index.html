How Colombia makes its electricity

Colombia generated about 90 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 70% came from hydro, and about 15% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 190 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 20% to about 15% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 5% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Colombia is a net importer: about 1% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 3 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Ecuador.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
