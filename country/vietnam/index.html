How Vietnam makes its electricity

Vietnam generated about 310 TWh of electricity in 2025. About half came from coal, and about a third from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 45% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Wind

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 460 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal rose from about 10% to about half, hydro fell from about 55% to about a third and other fossil fuels fell from about 15% to almost nothing of the mix.

Trade and resources

Vietnam is a net importer: about 1% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: China, Cambodia.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Korea.
