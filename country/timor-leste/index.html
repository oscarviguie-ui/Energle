How Timor-Leste makes its electricity

Timor-Leste generated about 0.5 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from other fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 670 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2003

The mix has changed little since 2003.

Trade and resources

Timor-Leste makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
