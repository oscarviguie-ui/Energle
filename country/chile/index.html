How Chile makes its electricity

Chile generated about 88 TWh of electricity in 2025. About a quarter came from solar, and about 20% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about two-thirds of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses a little more than the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 290 grams of CO₂, about two-thirds of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about half to about 20%, solar rose from almost nothing to about a quarter and wind rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Chile makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
