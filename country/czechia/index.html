How Czechia makes its electricity

Czechia makes its electricity mainly from nuclear and coal. Bioenergy and solar add a smaller share. Roughly half of its power is low-carbon. Its electricity is about as carbon-intensive as the world average. Each person uses more electricity than the world average. It sells some of its electricity to its neighbours.

Over the past two decades, nuclear and bioenergy have grown, while coal has declined.
