How Czechia makes its electricity

Czechia generated about 76 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from nuclear, and about a third from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 60% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Nuclear
  • Coal
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Wind

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 400 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 70% to about a third, nuclear rose from about 20% to about 40% and bioenergy rose from almost nothing to about 8% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Czechia is a net exporter: it sells about 10% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • Sunshine: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Germany, Slovakia, Austria.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
