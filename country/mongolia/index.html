How Mongolia makes its electricity

Mongolia generated about 9.7 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 85% came from coal, and about 6% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 8% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Other Fossil
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Hydro

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 820 grams of CO₂, about twice the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from almost all to about 85% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 6% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Mongolia is a net importer: about 20% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a fifth of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: China.
  • Main fuel supplier: China.
