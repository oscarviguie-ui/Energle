How Trinidad and Tobago makes its electricity

Trinidad and Tobago generated about 9.6 TWh of electricity in 2024. Almost all of it came from gas.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 680 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Trinidad and Tobago makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
