How Sudan makes its electricity

Sudan generated about 15 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 80% came from hydro, and about 20% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 80% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 150 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro rose from about 45% to about 80% and other fossil fuels fell from about half to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Sudan is a net importer: about 5% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 90% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Cyprus.
