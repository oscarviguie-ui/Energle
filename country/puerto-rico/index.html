How Puerto Rico makes its electricity

Puerto Rico generated about 20 TWh of electricity in 2025. About half came from other fossil fuels, and about 30% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 6% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Other Fossil
  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Wind

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 650 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 90% to about half, gas rose from about 8% to about 30% and coal rose from about 2% to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Puerto Rico makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
