How Tanzania makes its electricity

Tanzania generated about 8.8 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 65% came from gas, and about a third from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a third of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 340 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from almost nothing to about 65%, hydro fell from about 85% to about a third and other fossil fuels fell from about 10% to about 4% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Tanzania is a net importer: about 2% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 85% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Uganda, Zambia.
  • Main fuel supplier: India.
