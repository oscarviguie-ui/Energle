How Nicaragua makes its electricity

Nicaragua generated about 4.5 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 40% came from other fossil fuels, and about 20% from bioenergy. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 60% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Renewables
  • Hydro

Each person uses about a fifth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 300 grams of CO₂, about two-thirds of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 80% to about 40%, bioenergy rose from about 7% to about 20% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Nicaragua is a net importer: about 15% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Guatemala.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
