How Kuwait makes its electricity

Kuwait generated about 89 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 60% came from gas, and about a third from other fossil fuels. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Wind

Each person uses about 4.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 630 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about two-thirds to about a third and gas rose from about a third to about 60% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Kuwait makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a tenth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Nigeria.
