How Guinea makes its electricity

Guinea generated about 4 TWh of electricity in 2024. About three-quarters came from hydro, and about a quarter from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 180 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about half to about a quarter and hydro rose from about half to about three-quarters of the mix.

Trade and resources

Guinea makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Belgium.
