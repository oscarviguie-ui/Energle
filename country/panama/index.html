How Panama makes its electricity

Panama generated about 13 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 55% came from hydro, and about 20% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about two-thirds of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Coal

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 220 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 30% to about 7%, gas rose from almost nothing to about 20% and hydro fell from about 70% to about 55% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Panama is a net exporter: it sells about 1% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 100% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 2.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Guatemala, El Salvador.
  • Main fuel supplier: Ecuador.
