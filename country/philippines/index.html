How the Philippines makes its electricity

The Philippines generated about 120 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 60% came from coal, and about 15% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a quarter of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Other Renewables
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Bioenergy
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about a quarter of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 600 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal rose from about 35% to about 60%, other fossil fuels fell from about 20% to almost nothing and other renewables fell from about a quarter to about 8% of the mix.

Trade and resources

The Philippines makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about twice the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Korea.
