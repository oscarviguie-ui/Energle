How Nigeria makes its electricity

Nigeria generated about 42 TWh of electricity in 2025. About two-thirds came from gas, and about a third from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a third of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Hydro

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 460 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about 40% to about a third and gas rose from about 60% to about two-thirds of the mix.

Trade and resources

Nigeria is a net exporter: it sells about 6% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 85% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Benin, Togo, Niger.
  • Main fuel supplier: Belgium.
