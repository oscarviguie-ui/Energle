How Latvia makes its electricity

Latvia generated about 6.3 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 45% came from hydro, and about a quarter from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy
  • Wind

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 130 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about two-thirds to about 45%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 15% and bioenergy rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Latvia is a net importer: about 20% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about half the world average.
  • Rainfall: about two-thirds of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Estonia, Lithuania, Russia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Lithuania.
