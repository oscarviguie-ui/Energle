How Thailand makes its electricity

Thailand generated about 190 TWh of electricity in 2025. About two-thirds came from gas, and about 20% from coal. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Coal
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar
  • Hydro
  • Wind

Each person uses about three-quarters of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 550 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 10% to almost nothing and solar rose from almost nothing to about 5% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Thailand is a net importer: about 15% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Laos, Cambodia, Myanmar.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United Arab Emirates.
