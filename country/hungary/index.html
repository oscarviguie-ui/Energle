How Hungary makes its electricity

Hungary generated about 40 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from nuclear, and about a quarter from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Nuclear
  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Bioenergy
  • Coal
  • Wind
  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 160 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, solar rose from almost nothing to about a quarter, coal fell from about a quarter to about 3% and other fossil fuels fell from about 15% to almost nothing of the mix.

Trade and resources

Hungary is a net importer: about 20% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Slovakia, Austria, Croatia.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
