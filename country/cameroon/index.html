How Cameroon makes its electricity

Cameroon generated about 7.3 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 70% came from hydro, and about 20% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 230 grams of CO₂, about half the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about 70% and gas rose from almost nothing to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Cameroon makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Belgium.
