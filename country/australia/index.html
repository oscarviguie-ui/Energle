How Australia makes its electricity

Australia generated about 290 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 45% came from coal, and about 20% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 40% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Solar
  • Gas
  • Wind
  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about 3 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 520 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, coal fell from about 85% to about 45%, solar rose from almost nothing to about 20% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Australia makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Korea.
