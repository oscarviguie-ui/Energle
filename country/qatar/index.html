How Qatar makes its electricity

Qatar generated about 56 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 95% came from gas. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Solar

Each person uses about 4.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 580 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Qatar makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: less than a tenth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Kuwait.
