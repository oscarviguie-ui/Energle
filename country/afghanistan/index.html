How Afghanistan makes its electricity

Afghanistan generated about 1 TWh of electricity in 2024. About three-quarters came from hydro, and about 10% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Other Fossil
  • Coal

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 130 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about a third to about 10%, hydro rose from about two-thirds to about three-quarters and solar rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Afghanistan is a net importer: about 85% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Uzbekistan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Uzbekistan.
