How Papua New Guinea makes its electricity

Papua New Guinea generated about 4.7 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 55% came from other fossil fuels, and about 20% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a quarter of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Other Renewables

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 510 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels rose from about a third to about 55% and hydro fell from about 40% to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Papua New Guinea makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 65% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about 3 times the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Singapore.
