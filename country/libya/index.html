How Libya makes its electricity

Libya generated about 35 TWh of electricity in 2024. About three-quarters came from gas, and about a quarter from other fossil fuels. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses a little more than the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 830 grams of CO₂, about twice the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 80% to about a quarter and gas rose from about 20% to about three-quarters of the mix.

Trade and resources

Libya makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: less than a tenth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Italy.
