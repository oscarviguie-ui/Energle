How Croatia makes its electricity

Croatia generated about 15 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 40% came from hydro, and about 20% from wind. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about three-quarters of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy
  • Coal

Each person uses about 1.5 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 160 grams of CO₂, about a third of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, wind rose from almost nothing to about 20%, hydro fell from about 60% to about 40% and other fossil fuels fell from about 15% to almost nothing of the mix.

Trade and resources

Croatia is a net importer: about 25% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Hungary, Slovenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina.
  • Main fuel supplier: Italy.
