How Somalia makes its electricity

Somalia generated about 0.4 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 80% came from other fossil fuels, and about 20% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Wind

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 510 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about 80% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 20% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Somalia makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 75% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
