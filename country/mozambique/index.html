How Mozambique makes its electricity

Mozambique generated about 20 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 80% came from hydro, and about 15% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 85% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 130 grams of CO₂, about a quarter of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about 80% and gas rose from almost nothing to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Mozambique is a net exporter: it sells about 15% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 80% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe.
  • Main fuel supplier: Oman.
