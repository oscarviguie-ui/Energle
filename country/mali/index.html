How Mali makes its electricity

Mali generated about 6 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 80% came from other fossil fuels, and about 15% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 20% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 540 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels rose from about three-quarters to about 80% and hydro fell from about 20% to about 15% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Mali is a net exporter: it sells about 8% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 90% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Côte d'Ivoire.
  • Main fuel supplier: Côte d'Ivoire.
