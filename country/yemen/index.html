How Yemen makes its electricity

Yemen generated about 5.2 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 90% came from other fossil fuels, and about 10% from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 10% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Gas

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 590 grams of CO₂, about 1.5 times the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from almost all to about 90% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Yemen makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a tenth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Africa.
