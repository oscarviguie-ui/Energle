How Malawi makes its electricity

Malawi generated about 1.8 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 90% came from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 95% of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Other Fossil
  • Bioenergy
  • Solar

Each person uses less than a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 50 grams of CO₂, about a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro rose from about 85% to about 90% and other fossil fuels fell from about 10% to about 4% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Malawi is a net exporter: it sells about 1% of what it generates to its neighbours.

  • About 60% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: a little more than the world average.
  • Rainfall: close to the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: Tanzania.
