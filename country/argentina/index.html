How Argentina makes its electricity

Argentina generated about 150 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 55% came from gas, and about 15% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 40% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Nuclear
  • Other Fossil
  • Solar
  • Bioenergy
  • Coal

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 340 grams of CO₂, about three-quarters of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from about a third to about 15% and wind rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Argentina is a net importer: about 6% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United States.
