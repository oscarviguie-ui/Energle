How Mauritania makes its electricity

Mauritania generated about 2.1 TWh of electricity in 2024. About three-quarters came from other fossil fuels, and about 10% from hydro. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a quarter of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Wind

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 510 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 95% to about three-quarters and solar rose from almost nothing to about 8% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Mauritania is a net importer: about 15% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 95% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: less than a tenth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United Arab Emirates.
