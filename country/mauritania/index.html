How Mauritania makes its electricity

Mauritania makes most of its electricity from other fossil fuels, with hydro a distant second. Solar adds a smaller share. Fossil fuels still supply most of its power. Its electricity is about as carbon-intensive as the world average. Each person uses far less electricity than the world average. It buys some of its electricity from its neighbours.

Over the past two decades, solar has grown from almost nothing, while other fossil fuels have declined.
