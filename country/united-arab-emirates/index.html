How the United Arab Emirates makes its electricity

The United Arab Emirates generated about 180 TWh of electricity in 2024. About two-thirds came from gas, and about a quarter from nuclear. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about a third of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Gas
  • Nuclear
  • Solar

Each person uses about 4 times the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 470 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from almost all to about two-thirds, nuclear rose from almost nothing to about a quarter and solar rose from almost nothing to about 9% of the mix.

Trade and resources

The United Arab Emirates makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: less than a tenth of the world average.
  • Main fuel supplier: India.
