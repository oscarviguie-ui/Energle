How Egypt makes its electricity

Egypt generated about 250 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 80% came from gas, and about 7% from other fossil fuels. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 15% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Other Fossil
  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Solar

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 560 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas rose from about 60% to about 80%, other fossil fuels fell from about a quarter to about 7% and hydro fell from about 20% to about 6% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Egypt makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: less than a tenth of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Libya, Jordan.
  • Main fuel supplier: Saudi Arabia.
