How Turkey makes its electricity

Turkey generated about 350 TWh of electricity in 2025. About a third came from coal, and about 20% from gas. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied about 45% of the total.

The mix in 2025

  • Coal
  • Gas
  • Hydro
  • Wind
  • Solar
  • Other Renewables
  • Bioenergy

Each person uses close to the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 480 grams of CO₂, close to the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, gas fell from about 35% to about 20%, wind rose from almost nothing to about 10% and solar rose from almost nothing to about 10% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Turkey makes roughly as much electricity as it uses, with little net trade.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about half the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Bulgaria, Greece.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
