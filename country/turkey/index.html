How Turkey makes its electricity

Turkey makes its electricity mainly from coal and gas. Hydro and wind add a smaller share. Roughly half of its power is low-carbon. Its electricity is about as carbon-intensive as the world average. Each person uses about as much electricity as the world average. It makes about as much electricity as it uses.

Over the past two decades, wind and solar have grown from almost nothing, while gas and hydro have declined.
