How Lesotho makes its electricity

Lesotho generated about 0.5 TWh of electricity in 2022. Almost all of it came from hydro.

The mix in 2022

  • Hydro

Each person uses about a tenth of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 20 grams of CO₂, less than a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

The mix has changed little since 2000.

Trade and resources

Lesotho is a net importer: about 45% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 85% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about three-quarters of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: South Africa, Mozambique.
  • Main fuel supplier: South Africa.
