How Tunisia makes its electricity

Tunisia generated about 22 TWh of electricity in 2025. About 95% came from gas. Almost all of it came from fossil fuels.

The mix in 2025

  • Gas
  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Other Fossil

Each person uses about half the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 560 grams of CO₂, a little more than the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, other fossil fuels fell from about 10% to about 1% and gas rose from about 90% to about 95% of the mix.

Trade and resources

Tunisia is a net importer: about 10% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • Sunshine: close to the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a fifth of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: Algeria.
  • Main fuel supplier: Russia.
