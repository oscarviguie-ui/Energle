How Namibia makes its electricity

Namibia generated about 1.6 TWh of electricity in 2024. About 60% came from hydro, and about a third from solar. Low-carbon sources (nuclear and renewables) supplied almost all of the total.

The mix in 2024

  • Hydro
  • Solar
  • Coal
  • Wind

Each person uses about a third of the world average amount of electricity. Each kilowatt-hour emits about 50 grams of CO₂, about a tenth of the world average.

Since 2000

Since 2000, hydro fell from almost all to about 60% and solar rose from almost nothing to about a third of the mix.

Trade and resources

Namibia is a net importer: about 60% of the electricity it uses comes from abroad.

  • About 75% of people have electricity at home.
  • Sunshine: about 1.5 times the world average.
  • Rainfall: about a quarter of the world average.
  • Biggest electricity trade partners: South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe.
  • Main fuel supplier: the United Arab Emirates.
