Energle, the daily energy game

Every day, one country hides behind its electricity: how much power it makes, how it makes it, and what it trades with its neighbours. You have six guesses to name it. Energle is free, takes a few minutes, and teaches you something about the world’s energy each time you play.

How to play

  1. Read the country’s energy profile. How much electricity it generated and how much each person uses, compared with the world average; its net imports or exports; its power mix since 2000, source by source (coal, gas, nuclear, hydro, wind, solar, bioenergy and more); and how much CO₂ each kilowatt-hour emits.
  2. Guess a country. Type its name, or tap it on the map. After a wrong guess you get the distance to the answer, an arrow pointing toward it, and a colour that runs from red (far) to green (close).
  3. Stuck? Buy a clue. At any point, even before your first guess, the Clue Shop trades one guess for a clue card (see below).
  4. Share your result without spoiling the answer, and come back tomorrow: a new country arrives every day at midnight UTC.

The Clue Shop

Each clue card costs one guess and brings a new angle on the country. If a card has no data for that country, it’s free, and that’s a clue in itself.

Explore every country on a globe

Once you have played the daily puzzle, Explore opens: an interactive globe with every country’s full energy profile and clue cards. Add up a continent, the European Union or OPEC as if it were one country, pick a trait like the greenest grids or the biggest importers, or click a source such as nuclear, coal or solar to colour the world by how much of each country’s power it makes. You can also practise on any group of countries.

Want to read about one country? Electricity by country has a page for each of them: its power mix, how it has changed since 2000, and what it trades with its neighbours.

Archive, stats and leaderboard

Every past puzzle stays playable in the Archive (replays don’t count toward your stats). Your stats keep your streak and how many guesses you usually need. The leaderboard ranks today’s players by guesses, and all-time players by a weighted score that rewards both skill and regular play.

The data behind Energle

Energle is built entirely on open data, stitched into one profile per country and refreshed automatically every month.

Questions

Is Energle free?
Yes. There is no sign-up and no download: Energle works in any browser, on a phone or a computer.
When is there a new puzzle?
Every day at midnight UTC. Every past puzzle stays playable in the Archive.
How are the hints worked out?
After a wrong guess, Energle shows the distance between the centres of the two countries, an arrow pointing from your guess toward the answer, and a colour that runs from red when you are far to green when you are close.
How is the leaderboard ranked?
Today’s leaderboard ranks by the number of guesses, a missed puzzle counting as seven. The all-time leaderboard uses a weighted score: your average number of guesses, pulled toward the typical player’s until you have played enough puzzles for your own average to speak for itself. Lower is better.
Can I change my name on the leaderboard?
Yes: open Stats and choose Change name. Your scores, rank and streak move to the new name. It works from the device you first played on.
Who is it for?
Anyone curious about energy, geography or climate: students, teachers, energy professionals and puzzle fans.

Who makes Energle

Energle is made by Oscar Viguié, a student in energy economics and management at IFP School in France, as a fun way to learn about the world’s energy and the energy transition. Ideas and bug reports are welcome through the Feedback button in the game.
